## Concept

Sky Sanctuary GS is a stylized fantasy environment made of floating islands,
suspended bridges, celestial cypress trees, translucent blue crystals, and a
monumental open-air altar. The camera approaches through the island chain, passes the
bridge, and circles the sanctuary so the scene is experienced as a full 3D
environment rather than a single framed view.

## AI Method

The project uses a compact PyTorch residual material network to learn the appearance
attributes of a dense Gaussian-splat scene. The network receives splat position,
surface normal, authored base color, and material identity, then predicts lighting-
aware residual RGB, opacity, and emissive values. Its learned attributes are stored in
the final Gaussian scene and used by the renderer.

The scene also saves synthetic multi-view teacher images for visual inspection. The
teacher is an original procedural scene created by this project; no external 3D asset
or pretrained model is used.

## 3D Representation

The submitted 3D content is a binary PLY containing tangent-aligned anisotropic
Gaussians. Each splat stores:

- Position and surface normal
- Spherical-harmonic DC color and explicit RGB
- Three log scales and a quaternion rotation
- Logit opacity and explicit alpha
- Emissive strength and material ID

Splats are sampled according to triangle area and material importance. Their tangent-
plane scales overlap enough to cover surfaces, avoiding the isolated-point appearance
of a sparse point cloud.

## Textures and Lighting

All material textures are generated by project code as high-resolution RGBA fields:

- Teal painterly moss and grass
- Layered blue-green cypress foliage
- Layered indigo floating rock
- Carved blue-gray celestial stone
- Aged engraved gold
- Long-grain bridge wood
- Faceted translucent cyan crystals
- Soft low cloud banks

Crystals carry real alpha values in the material texture, learned Gaussian attributes,
PLY export, and custom renderer. Their learned emissive values create bloom and local
cyan illumination on nearby surfaces. A directional night key light, cool fill light,
and altar-centered light field provide dramatic form while retaining the stylized
fantasy palette.

The altar is built from explicit staggered masonry courses over a dark grout core.
Its large high-contrast stone texture and extra per-face Gaussian sampling keep the
brick pattern legible after splat blending. A larger roof-and-pillar structure was
removed so the altar and trees have a cleaner silhouette.

## Rendering

The final video is rendered entirely by the custom NumPy Gaussian-splat renderer in
`src/pipeline.py`. No Blender or third-party 3D rendering software is used.

Opaque splats are resolved as normalized Gaussian surfaces within the first visible
depth layer. This fills complete surfaces while preserving texture detail and keeping
separate objects from blending together. Crystals and clouds are rendered in a
back-to-front alpha pass, followed by a small emissive bloom pass.

## Reproduction

```bash
chmod +x setup.sh run.sh
./setup.sh
./run.sh
```

`setup.sh` creates the dedicated Python 3.10 conda environment and installs all
Python dependencies with `pip`.
